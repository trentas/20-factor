How to use

Tick the items you have in place, and mark items that don't apply as N/A. Each factor shows its declared coverage: the share of its applicable items you ticked. This is a self-assessment, not a measured maturity level — it says nothing about evidence or how well a control works. N/A items are left out, and a factor whose items are all N/A has no score and is left out of the totals.

Answers are saved in your browser and tied to the exact wording of each item. When an item is reworded, its answer is kept but marked for review until you confirm it. Export the JSON to share or back up.
